# Privacy Policy for NoTabNotif

**Last updated:** June 2026

NoTabNotif does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

- No analytics
- No tracking
- No remote code
- No data collection of any kind

All settings (whitelist, blacklist, mode) are stored locally on your device using browser.storage.sync. These settings never leave your browser.

## Permissions explained:
- `tabs`: Used to read and modify tab titles
- `storage`: Used to save your whitelist/blacklist preferences
- `<all_urls>`: Required to run on any website you choose to enable the extension on
